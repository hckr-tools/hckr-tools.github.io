# 🔀 Diff Checker & Comparison

**Component**: `src/sidepanel/tools/DiffChecker.tsx`  
**Styles**: `src/sidepanel/tools/DiffChecker.css`

## Overview

Compare two blocks of code, JSON, or text with fine-grained visual highlighting.

## Features

- **Split View & Unified View**: Toggle between dual-column side-by-side comparison and Git-style unified diff.
- **Granular Highlighting**: Identifies added lines (green), removed lines (red), and inline character-level mutations.
- **Whitespace & Case Options**: Toggle options to ignore trailing whitespace or ignore casing differences.
- **Swap Inputs**: 1-click button to flip Left and Right panes.
